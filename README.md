# 🔐 KeeCrypt

> **Your data. Your device. Your control.**

KeeCrypt is a **privacy-first, offline vault** designed to securely store passwords and other sensitive information on your device.

No account. No cloud storage. No ads. No tracking.

Your data stays under your control.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🔒 **Secure Local Vault** - Store sensitive information securely on your device.
* 🧩 **Custom Fields** - Add your own fields to any entry for storing different types of information.
* 🔑 **Master Password** - Protect your entire vault with a master password.
* 👆 **Biometric Unlock** - Optionally unlock your vault using device biometrics.
* 🔍 **Quick Search** - Search saved entries by app or website name.
* 📂 **Custom Categories** - Create, rename, delete, and reorder categories.
* ⭐ **Favorites** - Mark important apps and websites for easier access.
* 👁️ **Show/Hide Passwords** - Reveal sensitive values only when needed.
* 💾 **Encrypted Backup** - Export your vault as an encrypted JSON backup.
* 📄 **Text Export** - Export your information as a readable TXT file for viewing.
* 🔄 **Restore Backup** - Restore your data using an encrypted KeeCrypt JSON backup.
* 🌙 **Light & Dark Mode** - Choose Light, Dark, or System Default.
* 🔐 **Auto Lock** - Automatically lock your vault after a selected period.
* 📴 **Offline First** - Designed to work without relying on cloud services.

## 🛡️ Privacy First

KeeCrypt is built around a simple idea:

> **Your sensitive data should belong to you.**

KeeCrypt does not require:

* ❌ An account
* ❌ Cloud storage
* ❌ Advertisements
* ❌ Tracking
* ❌ Analytics

Your vault is designed to remain locally stored on your device.

---

## 📦 Backup & Restore

KeeCrypt provides two export formats:

### 🔒 Encrypted JSON

Used for:

* Backing up your vault
* Moving your data to another device
* Restoring your vault

Only encrypted JSON backups can be imported back into KeeCrypt.

### 📄 TXT

Used for:

* Viewing your saved information
* Keeping a readable copy

**TXT files cannot be imported back into KeeCrypt.**

> ⚠️ Keep exported files secure because they may contain sensitive information.

## 🧩 Custom Fields

KeeCrypt isn't limited to usernames and passwords.

Every entry can contain custom fields created by the user.

For example:

```text
Google
├── Username: example@gmail.com
├── Password: ••••••••••
├── Recovery Email: example@outlook.com
├── Recovery Code: ••••••••••
└── Custom Field: Any information
```

This allows KeeCrypt to store many types of sensitive information in one secure entry.

## 🔐 Security

KeeCrypt uses a master password to protect access to your vault.

Sensitive information should never be stored or displayed unnecessarily.

Biometric authentication is optional and can be enabled or disabled from Settings.

The vault can also automatically lock after a period of inactivity.
